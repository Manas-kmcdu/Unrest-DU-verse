Child Safety Standards

Effective date: June 2026

Unrest is a verified campus network for Delhi University students. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Zero tolerance

We prohibit any content or conduct that exploits, sexualizes, or endangers minors. This includes CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, and any attempt to use Unrest to contact minors for sexual purposes.

2. Eligibility and audience

Unrest is intended for university students with verified campus affiliation. We do not knowingly permit use by children under 13. Accounts that appear to belong to minors or that violate these standards are removed.

3. Prevention measures

  • Verified sign-in for Delhi University students and moderated onboarding for other allowlisted accounts.
  • Human moderation of user-generated posts, profiles, and reported content.
  • In-app reporting on posts and user blocking in Corridor chats.
  • Account suspension or permanent removal for policy violations.

4. Reporting in the app

Users can report posts from the post menu. Moderators review reports and may remove content, warn users, or ban accounts. In Corridor, users can report or block other participants from the in-chat safety controls.

5. Reporting to Unrest

If you believe content on Unrest involves CSAE or CSAM, contact us immediately at contact@unrestdu.in with the username, post link or description, and screenshots if available. We prioritize these reports for urgent review.

6. Law enforcement cooperation

When we identify CSAM or credible CSAE risk, we remove the content, disable the account, preserve relevant records as required by law, and report to appropriate authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or local law enforcement where applicable.

7. Moderator training and enforcement

Our moderation team is instructed to escalate any suspected CSAM or CSAE immediately, preserve evidence, and not re-expose harmful material during review. Repeat or severe violations result in permanent account removal.

8. Contact for Google Play and regulators

Designated child safety contact: contact@unrestdu.in (alternate: manaspandeya@gmail.com). This contact can respond to questions about our CSAE prevention practices and compliance.
