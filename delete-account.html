Delete Your Unrest Account

This page explains how to request account deletion for the Unrest app and what happens to your data after deletion.

Last updated: 2026-06-01

How to request deletion

In-app method (recommended)

Open Unrest → Profile → Settings → Delete Account, then confirm the request from within the app.

Backup method (if you cannot log in)

Email us from your registered account at contact@unrestdu.in with subject Delete my Unrest account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and authentication linkage.
  • Your personal data associated with your user account.
  • Your uploaded profile media and related account artifacts where applicable.

What may be retained temporarily

  • Limited records required for legal, fraud-prevention, or security obligations.
  • Operational logs and backups for a limited retention period.

Deletion timeline

We start processing deletion requests promptly. Most account deletions are completed within 30 days, subject to legal or security exceptions.

Need help?
